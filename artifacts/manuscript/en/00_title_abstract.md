## Title

**Identify, Predict, Actuate: Safe VPS Density Maximization under Censored Demand, Phase Structure, and Domain-Scoped No-Regression Constraints**

(alternate): *No-Regression Resource Efficiency for Small VPS Hosts: Censoring Identifiability, the Density Law, and Precision Control*

## Abstract

Small VPS providers pack many cheap, mostly-idle, occasionally-bursty tenants onto few physical cores; oversubscription is their business model. We recast "safe oversubscription" as three ordered problems. **Identify**: demand behind quota censoring is unobservable — a usage dashboard shows the quota, not the demand. We give an identifiability analysis (the point estimate equals the mean over the running stretch; a conditional floor $q(1{+}t)$ under the (aggregate-unverifiable) everywhere-saturated assumption; an explicit aggregate non-identifiability construction) and validate it across 180 cycles (900 tenant-windows; two batches); naively "releasing quotas on visible headroom" violates the SLO in 30 independent rounds (90 time-correlated cycles, clustered by round). **Predict**: capacity is set by phase structure, not average utilization. We propose the density-law **approximation** $N^* \approx C/(\rho\kappa)$ ($\kappa$ = measurable phase concentration; explicit assumptions, honest error directions) and validate it on a 4-vCPU nested bed and a 96-thread bare-metal host: a 3.5x safe-capacity ratio between random and synchronized phases (aligned 8 invariant across aggregation conventions, random 28→26 under the strictest; 3.5x–4x across quality bars), structural-phase break points hit (predicted 13.3/10.0 → measured 13/16 and 10/13; the random end is tail-optimistic and corrected by the measured bar), and an impossibility counterexample (a 35%-busy set collapses while a 98%-busy set stays safe). **Organizing thesis.** The bottleneck of safe-capacity decisions is observational capability: censoring identification, phase resolution (joint structure) and time resolution (sampling rate) are **each necessary** (three constructive counterexamples, Section 3.6) and jointly sufficient for our target workload family (an empirical statement) — our admission controller implements that observation set and is the only policy in the complete 14-round showdown that combines competitive random-phase capacity (16–20 seats, customer P5 ≥ 70%) with the conservative aligned-phase stop inside the verified bracket (8 seats; customer P5 ≈ 99%, 4-round ladder). **Actuate**: domain-scoped no-regression precision control — phase-aware admission lands on the fine-scanned aligned-phase bracket ([8,10); 8 seats), with 4/5 zero over-admission (the one optimistic case is blocked by the online gate), while component ablations and admitted-VM baselines (worst-case quota, quota-scaled, AUDIBLE-style Monte Carlo) either oversell or waste 2–3× capacity; on the memory axis, balloon-free reclaim (KSM dedup, free-page reporting, zram) recovers 33% of fleet RAM with zero tenant-visible change, and idle "1 GB" VMs hold only ~0.2–0.3 GB of host memory. All claims rest on mass repeats (100/180/30-round campaigns), two testbeds, topology-verified physical isolation, and read-only production telemetry.
