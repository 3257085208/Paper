# Declarations (JNSM)

**Data availability.** All code, raw traces and analysis scripts are released in the paper repository
(github.com/3257085208/Paper): `artifacts/tools/` (every experiment driver and analysis script, including
executable counterexample checks and the P5 convention analysis), `artifacts/research_data/` (raw JSONL
and per-request probe files, ~62 MB), `artifacts/figs/` (figure scripts) and `REPRODUCE.md` (one-minute
checks). Production telemetry was collected read-only on infrastructure we operate, anonymized (HMAC'd
identifiers) and is released only in aggregate form; no user-identifying data is included.

**Competing interests.** The author declares no competing interests.

**Ethics.** No human or animal subjects; the production telemetry involves no personal data (anonymized,
aggregate-only).

**Funding.** Not applicable.

**Author contributions.** Single-author work: conceptualization, methodology, software, experiments,
analysis, writing — Kaixiang Nie.

**Use of AI tools.** AI assistants were used for language editing and code review during preparation;
all research design, experiments and scientific claims are the author's own.
